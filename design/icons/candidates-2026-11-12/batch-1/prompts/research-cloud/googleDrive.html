    Productivity

Google Drive lets you securely store, intelligently organize and collaborate on files and folders from anywhere, on any device. With Drive, you can: • Safely store and access your files anywhere • Edit and store 100+ file types, including PDFs, Office files, videos and more • Scan and upload paper documents using your device’s camera • Quickly access recent and important files • Search for files by name and content • Filter files by type, last modified date and more • Share and set permissions for files and folders • View your content on the go while offline • Receive notifications about important activity on your files Google Workspace subscribers have access to additional Drive functionality, including: • Using AI to summarize information, retrieve quick facts, and surface insights from your files • Security and management controls for admins to help meet data compliance needs • Sharing files and folders directly with groups or teams within your organization • Creating a shared drive to store all of your team’s content • Requesting electronic signatures and manage vendor agreements, customer contracts, and more Learn more about Google Apps update policy: https://support.google.com/a/answer/6288871 Google accounts get 15GB of storage, shared across Google Drive, Gmail, and Google Photos. For additional storage, you can upgrade to Google Workspace or Google One as an in-app purchase. Storage subscriptions purchased from the app will be charged to your iTunes Account, and automatically renew unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Subscriptions and auto-renewal may be managed by going to iTunes Account Settings after purchase.   • Marvelous Memories
    08/03/2023
    Life with CeCe

    We recently had to part with our beloved little dog, who we think was probably near 17 years old. We had her for 11+ years; her first 5+ years were spent in a puppy mill. Once she learned what being loved is, she learned to play, make up games on her own, and fill our home with her loving presence. Losing her was and still is extremely painful. Our daughter gathered all the videos that we had collected over the years. Having these visual reminders of our beloved CeCe is so comforting. It will take many years to think of her without tears, but the videos and photos help soothe our hearts. They give us visual experience of the good and fun times, which were many! Her running circles in the backyard, just celebrating the joy of being free and loved, are treasures. Giving her first trip to the beach was a treat, as we watch her try to dog paddle in the surf that she was seeing for the first time! She never was afraid as long as one of us was within visual or hearing distance. The collection of all the years we had her is priceless, especially in those moments when her absence is so difficult. Thank you for giving a platform where we can collect our most loved moments with our girl. I especially, who spent the most time with her, am so grateful to be able to click on your icon and go to a happy place! This is a wonderful gift for all of life’s memories, captured for viewing whenever we want or need to see our girl again. Thank you so much for this treasure!

  • Saved my pictures I thought I lost forever
    10/03/2018
    soojlisa

    I’m writing this review to thank Google! A few years back, I was syncing my new MacBook and somehow ended up erasing ALL my pictures. I was so distraught and it took me sometime to finally accept it. So, yesterday, I’m on Google Drive on one of my gmail accounts, when I have the idea to just check my Trash; of course it has been over three years and I’m not getting any of my hopes up. WELL! Omg, I see that in the trash are ALL my pics that were deleted! I was beyond ecstatic, like finding lost treasure or something! Not to be totally dramatic, but losing ALL your memories is a bit of a tiny trauma! It has happened twice before, both times due to using Apple products! The first time was due to that iCloud fiasco few years back where we all thought syncing meant that Apple was backing up all our stuff to the cloud but that wasn’t it at all! So bam, lost a TON of pics from the two best summers of my life! And then when I bought a new MacBook I was trying to “sync” my photos to Google, not trusting the Apple ICloud anymore, and again I erase literally ten-fifteen years worth of pics ! So anyway, when I found these pics it was like getting back all these great memories and I can’t thank Google Drive enough!!! I mean most Trash just saves things for 30 days max but i just cannot believe my luck, and the fact that somehow I was backing up my stuff to Google Drive and not just Google Photos. It’s the best app guys, iTS LITERALLY THE BEST app to back up photos! Google rocks!

  • Surprisingly iOS Drive works better than Androids version
    05/20/2025
    TalysPops

    I’m shocked that iOS’s G-Drive is working better than Androids version. This is despite my iPhone 12 Max running on iOS 14.5.1, latest is iOS 18.3 (I think), Jailbroken so can’t update. My Pixel 6 is on A14, and have never seen the thumbnail images of my uploaded pics in Androids Drive. Had no idea that was a thing till I just opened iOS’s version and they all populated super quick. I had issues downloading .AVI video files on my Pixel, when I tried on iOS, zero issues. There’s no option to download those vids on iOS though, however, selecting “open in” I was able to choose my video player app, VLC and it looks like it was saved in that app’s Video List. If you’re wanting to download your saved vids in Drive, you may want to have a good video app installed prior to trying to download them. I’m pretty sure no other options like iOS’s stock “Photos” was an option, but that could be related to my me running 14.5.1. Big props to iOS Devs who did a much better job than Google did on their very own apps, using their very own device. I’m a Fan of both platforms, would never say one is better than the other, I truly believe each have their Pros & Cons, for that reason is why I carry both in my pockets everywhere I go and I’m content with that. Leaving home without either one feels like I forgot my left foot.

  • Google Wins
    05/12/2018
    SattireSam

    There are so many drives to choose from nowadays... Google, Outlook, ICloud, Dropbox, Zoho. While I do love Zoho, and think that, like google, they are a very tech savvy and innovated company that actually listens and takes advice from their users, I think Google Drive is probably the easiest to use. But- take notice that,if I didn’t have thousands of documents stored in google already, there’s a good chance I would’ve made the switch to Zoho. The main reason being storage limits. google used to offer ways to earn or win extra storage space, and now I don’t believe they do. Also, the storage included is smaller than most drives offer. The winning factor is that google photos will store unlimited pictures & if that wasn’t the case, I wouldn’t be able to use google at all. I have over 500gb of photos stored currently. I would like to see other ways to gain access to additional storage. There are people that refuse to purchase things that aren’t Physical objects, or people who refuse to put their banking into on the internet. Then there are people who simply can’t afford extras in life. I contribute a lot to all of googles programs, including Express & detailed reviews / data on google maps etc. That could be a way to reward people with storage space. I guess I’m saying that Zoho might be small, but they are on your heels. They offer these options & google needs to step-up. There are millions of people in the market for couponing which means that today’s internet users are cheap. Google isn’t going to go bankrupt by offering ways to earn free storage.
